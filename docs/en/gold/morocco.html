FAQ

Why does the price differ from the jeweler's?

Jewelers add making charges, tax and margin; the price here is raw gold computed from the global spot price.

What is the difference between karats?

Karat measures purity out of 24: 24K is nearly pure, 21K is 87.5% gold, 18K is 75%.

How often does the price update?

It refreshes automatically every few minutes while the page is open.
